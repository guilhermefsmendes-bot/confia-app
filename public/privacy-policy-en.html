Privacy Policy — CONFIA

Work-in-progress version: 2 November 2026

CONFIA — Know Yourself Better is an emotional wellbeing and self-care application. Its tools support reflection and self-regulation and do not replace diagnosis, treatment or care from health professionals.

1. Data controller

Controller: Guilherme F. S. Mendes, developer responsible for CONFIA. Privacy and rights requests: [insert and verify the official monitored contact address before publication].

2. Minimum age

CONFIA is intended exclusively for people aged 18 or older. It is not designed to intentionally collect data from minors. If you become aware of use by a minor, contact the controller so the relevant data can be assessed and deleted.

3. Data we process

Technical account data: an anonymous Firebase identifier used to maintain sessions and associate data with the same user/device; no traditional username/password account is required. Personal records: emotional ratings, check-ins, goals, habits, sleep, exercise, nutrition, experiments and notes you choose to save. Community data: posts, reactions, messages, poll votes, matching requests, circle participation, reports and blocks. Technical/usage data: preferences, language, notification state, notification tokens and local usage events needed for the experience. Share only what you choose; Community posts and messages are visible to the intended participants.

4. Purposes

To provide personal records, goals and self-regulation tools; generate descriptive observations from your own records without clinical diagnosis; support voluntary Community participation and matching; protect users through reports, blocks and moderation; store preferences and deliver authorized notifications; and maintain security, fix errors and improve reliability.

5. Storage and providers

CONFIA uses on-device storage and Firebase/Google Cloud for anonymous authentication, synchronization, Community and notifications. The project region, active services and processor terms must be confirmed by the controller and reflected here before publication. Personal Manual notes, experiments and product activation/return events are stored locally and are not sent to a remote analytics service in this version. If an external diagnostic collector is configured, minimal technical rendering-error data may be sent without diary text, messages, account IDs or personal content.

6. Sharing and visibility

Private records and goals are intended for your individual experience. Community content is shared with the users/groups covered by the feature. Technical providers and authorized moderators may process data only as needed for their roles. CONFIA does not sell personal data and must not use diary or message content for behavioral advertising.

7. Retention

Personal records and preferences are retained while needed to provide the experience or until deletion is requested, subject to temporary technical copies and legal duties. Messages and posts remain while the feature is active and until deleted by you, moderation or an account-deletion request, while respecting other participants’ content. Pending reports remain until reviewed; after a final moderation decision (reviewed, actioned or dismissed), they are scheduled for deletion after 180 days unless a legal/safety hold applies. Notification tokens, matching profiles and blocks created by you are deleted during account deletion. A block created by another person may remain as that person’s safety preference, linked to the technical identifier needed to prevent contact.

8. Safety and Community

Report and block controls are available. Peer listening is informal and is not a clinical service, crisis line or emergency response. The in-app SOS provides individual self-regulation tools; it does not contact third parties or send help requests. In immediate danger, contact local emergency services. The controller must review reports, document decisions, restrict access and follow incident procedures.

9. Rights and deletion

Under applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or object to processing, and may complain to the competent supervisory authority. “Delete my data” in Settings starts deletion of data associated with the app’s technical identity. Some safety records may be retained for the periods described above. Local-only data must be deleted on each device where it is stored. Uninstalling the app alone does not necessarily delete synchronized data.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated to reflect product, technical or legal changes. The version and last-updated date will appear on this page. For rights requests or questions, use the official contact in section 1.
